Simulated projection: steel block with a hydrogenous rod. Steel is nearly transparent to neutrons; hydrogen attenuates strongly. Blur is drawn 10× larger than Ug so it stays visible; noise grows as flux drops.

Not to scale. Component order and roles follow the RN2 component flowchart. The real flight path is about 1.5 m and evacuated; distances in the model are compressed so the whole line fits on screen.

Divergence. Beam spread in the animation follows D/L (exaggerated 10×), so a larger pinhole or a shorter flight path widens the cone and blurs the image.

Optics. The Gadox or ⁶LiF/ZnS(Ag) screen is the only conversion point. Its light goes to a 45° first-surface mirror, then horizontally to the cooled CMOS camera placed off the direct beam.
